03 — The person behind the camera

Holding space for the real.

For me, filmmaking is less about arriving with an answer and more about staying with a question. What is happening here? Who gets to speak? What changes when we pay attention?

Since 2019, I’ve worked across direction, cinematography, editing, and colour grading—often alongside grassroots communities and social-sector teams. Along the way, I’ve trained 300+ practitioners to make storytelling part of how their organisations work, not just how they report.
